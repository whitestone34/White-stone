About Us

White Stone Cafe is a family restaurant on GT Road in Awan Colony, Haripur, near Bank Al Habib. We serve grills, steaks, Chinese and Thai dishes, noodles, soups, appetizers and pizzas in a bright, colourful dining space.

Our grilled chicken and beef steaks are marinated in White Stone spices and flame grilled to your liking, with a sauce of your choice, served with fries and mash.
